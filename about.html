About MediConnect

Welcome to MediConnect, a nearby pharmacy finder website designed to help people locate pharmacies in unfamiliar areas.

Our Purpose

Finding a nearby pharmacy in an unfamiliar location can sometimes be difficult. MediConnect aims to make this process easier by helping users find pharmacies according to their preferred distance.

How MediConnect Works

Users can search for a medicine, choose their preferred distance or radius, and explore nearby pharmacies within that distance.

Our Scope

MediConnect focuses only on helping users find nearby pharmacies. It does not provide online medicine ordering, payments, delivery, diagnosis, or medical advice.
